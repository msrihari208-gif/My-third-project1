Admission Standards

Eligibility Criteria & Documentation

Review the minimum academic qualifications, accepted national/university entrance examinations, and required verification documents.

Minimum Marks & Qualification
  • B.Tech Programs: Passed 10+2 (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics) with at least 60% aggregate marks (55% for reserved categories).
  • BBA & BCA Programs: Passed 10+2 in any stream from a recognized board with minimum 50% aggregate marks (Mathematics/CS preferred for BCA).
  • MBA & MCA Programs: Recognized Bachelor’s degree (minimum 3 years) with at least 50% aggregate marks.
Entrance Exam Requirements
  • B.Tech (CSE / AI-ML / ECE): Valid score in JEE Main, State CET, or AGU-CET 2026 (University Entrance Test).
  • MBA: Valid score in CAT / MAT / XAT / CMAT / GMAT or AGU-MAT 2026 followed by Group Discussion & Personal Interview.
  • BBA / BCA / MCA: Merit-based evaluation of qualifying exam marks + Online Aptitude & Personal Interview.
Required Documents
  • Class 10th & Class 12th Official Marksheets & Certificates
  • Bachelor’s Degree Marksheets / Provisional Certificate (for PG)
  • Entrance Exam Scorecard (JEE / CAT / MAT / AGU-CET, if applicable)
  • Transfer Certificate (TC) & Migration Certificate
  • Government Photo ID (Aadhaar Card / Passport) & 4 Passport Photos
Course-Specific Eligibility Highlights
B.Tech (CSE, AI & ML, ECE)
Mandatory Physics & Mathematics in 10+2 along with Chemistry / Computer Science / Electronics.
MCA (Master of Computer Applications)
BCA / B.Sc. (CS/IT) or any graduate with Mathematics at 10+2 or graduation level (bridge course available).
BBA & MBA Management
Open to students from Science, Commerce, and Humanities backgrounds with strong analytical & communication skills.

For the 2026–2027 academic session, Aurelia Global University offers 7 flagship programs: B.Tech in Computer Science Engineering, B.Tech in Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning, B.Tech in Electronics & Communication Engineering, BBA, MBA, BCA, and MCA.

For B.Tech programs, candidates must have passed 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics with at least 60% marks along with JEE Main or AGU-CET scores. For BBA & BCA, 50% aggregate in 10+2 is required. For MBA & MCA, a recognized Bachelor’s degree with a minimum of 50% marks is mandatory.
